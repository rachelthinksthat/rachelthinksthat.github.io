Tabloid tall tale · LSU

NASA Takes Photo Of Ghosts In Space

So there I was flying along, minding my own business, I soared by Taurus and Perseus too and caught a glimpse of Sirius. I decided to take a rest and gaze at my favorite constellations, but I felt a presence at my back and froze as my heart hastened. I turned around and saw this thing unlike anything ever before. It was huge and weird and sharp and fast, and it was looking at me! I’m sure! Why it wanted to gaze at me a humble little ghost? I don’t know, but he looked so long I said “Take a picture and go!”
