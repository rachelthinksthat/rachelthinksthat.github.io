Mr. O Wellington

Mr. O Wellington lives in an old rickety house on the dead end of Everett Drive. He chose to live on Everett drive because rumors of supernatural activity have swirled around the road since its creation in the late eighteen hundreds. Mr. Wellington has had a fascination with all things supernatural ever since his grandfather’s sudden disappearance from his house when he was a little boy. His grandfather’s front door was wide open with huge claw marks trailing from the front door down to the edge of the woods on the left side of his house. Mr. Wellington worked as a freelance welder for fifty-five years until a tragic accident caused him to retire. Mr. Wellington was moving a black cabinet for his wife Evelyn when a loud strange noise suddenly burst through the open window causing him to miss a step on the stairs and lose balance. As Mr. Wellington fell he held onto his wife’s black cabinet and crushed the bones in his left hand with each tumble until his hand was too broken to keep his grip. He finally reached the bottom but the cabinet landed on top of him trapping him at the base of the stairs. Mrs. Wellington was at the grocery store when the accident happened, so Mr. Wellington could only lay and wait for help as his left hand swelled up to the size of a basketball. When his wife got home and found him she rushed him to the hospital, but the doctors said the damage to his hand became impossible to repair. Mr. Wellington spends his days in an uncomfortable recliner, staring out of the open window, and wondering what possibly could have made that strange noise. He imagines how his encounter with this strange noise maker would go. If it was an animal would he kill it? If it was a human would he murder it? Murder. No that was out of the question, but maybe he could find a way to sue them and get some money for all they had taken away from him. But what if it was something else… something supernatural. The day after his fall Mr. Wellington scoured his yard for sign of those claw marks, he tore through the woods yelling and cursing for the wretched beast to show itself and hoped he could conquer the beast if it revealed itself. He searched and shouted for weeks which turned to months, until finally he settled himself into that uncomfortable recliner, stared out the open window, and listened for that strange noise again unable to forget that fateful day. Mrs. Wellington nags Mr. Wellington to stop being so obsessed with that strange noise, and tells him to find a normal hobby like keeping the crabgrass off of their front lawn. Mr. Wellington couldn’t care less how much crabgrass is on the front lawn unless it is mutant crabgrass that can make that strange noise. There’s absolutely no way that could be the cause. Is there?
