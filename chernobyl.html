Documentary poem · LSU

Chernobyl Disaster

April 26, 1986

  1. two Chernobyl plant workers died on the night of the accident
  2. one of only two nuclear energy accidents
  3. a catastrophic nuclear accident
  4. the wind was warmer that day
  5. a chill was always in the air
  6. but today was the first warm day
  7. snow melts beneath my feet
  8. on the usual walk up to the gate
  9. beep. enter. repeat.
  10. been at it some three months
  11. dad said I needed work
  12. they trained me a week or two
  13. set me out on the line
  14. click. beeb. click. clank.
  15. easy day easy pay
  16. my line mate’s motto
  17. we operate reactor four
  18. there’s a simulation tonight, a station blackout
  19. we start the preparations
  20. my mate says just gotta arrange the core
  21. easy day easy pay
  22. we have been talking about the shutdown for weeks
  23. HOLD OFF ON THE SHUTDOWN the director has it postponed
  24. we finish our shift and head out for the day
  25. easy day easy pay
  26. shut down for the reactor delayed, a shut down meant for the day
  27. night shift workers arrive planning maintain decay for a shut-down plant
  28. unit shift chief in charge of the test itself
  29. takes over the shift at midnight
  30. he wants to abort the test
  31. the deputy chief engineer supervises the test
  32. insists they continue the test
  33. day shift workers long gone
  34. a shut down had been delayed
  35. disconnects the clutches to let the rods descend into the core
  36. rods do not move
  37. reactor four makes rumbling noises
  38. confusion
  39. unexpected consequences result from unexpected delays
  40. control panel indicates no water flow and failure of pumps
  41. 160 people on duty
  42. a radiation monitoring technician leans back in his chair
  43. Write down results for three and five. Four has been shut down. Check. Control. Monitor. Yawn—
  44. THUD. the monitors shake, the room sways,
  45. Sir, I believe it was a water hammer pulse from the shutdown—
  46. THUD. lights out, control panel for reactor four loses signal
  47. BLAST. latched double doors being blown apart
  48. black and red powder falling from the ventilation vent
  49. emergency lights switched on
  50. Sir! The telephone connection with Unit 4 was cut!
  51. corridor full of steam and white dust
  52. eyes widen… radiation counters have gone off-scale
  53. concrete litters the turbine hall How did this happen?
  54. debris from the crumbling building crashing down as a pump operator races to his superiors
  55. badly burned and drenched with poison
  56. vision tunneled by shock
  57. crying Save him save him! but the building no longer exists
  58. milky grey radioactive dust and steam lingers in the halls
  59. room 604, Vladimir Shashenok, the automatic systems adjuster, reports pressure readings on his instruments pre explosion
  60. gauges burst from overpressure spike
  61. mercury showers burn deep into Vladimir’s body
  62. blinding him with pain
  63. BLAST a beam pins Shashenok to the ground knocking him unconscious
  64. the power goes out
  65. boiling water and radioactive steam flow into the room
  66. the Chernobyl enterprise group supervisor makes his way to the turbine hall on orders from the unit shift chief
  67. passing fires on levels zero and twelve
  68. broken pipes of oil and water
  69. crushed-in roof debris
  70. scattered pieces of graphite and fuel
  71. linoleum burning around them
  72. an utter catastrophe
  73. attempts to contact Shashenok, room 604
  74. no answer
  75. he meets the radiation technician the Chief sent me to find you
  76. eyes exchange worry and pain
  77. faces stoned in determination
  78. We have been ordered to rescue Shashenok
  79. silent nods exchanged
  80. room 604, rescue team arrives
  81. Shashenok lays pinned under a beam unconscious
  82. bloody foam bubbling from his mouth
  83. soaked in radioactive water
  84. Shashenok’s hand burns radiation into the backs of his rescuers
  85. whose rescue was in vain
  86. died 6 a.m.
  87. the radiation technician returns to his post after changing his clothes and shoes
  88. ordered to search for the pump operator
  89. again a rescue in vain
  90. Valery Khodemchuk, circulating pump operator most likely killed immediately.
  91. crushed under the pump building. body never recovered.
  92. Khodemchuk and Shashenok were the first two victims of the disaster.
  93. firefighters arrive and hurry to extinguish fires
  94. kamikazes with a moral obligation
  95. put out the fire yet suffer radiation
  96. tastes of metal fill their tongues
  97. skins pricked with pins and needles
  98. townspeople go about business as usual until coughing fits kick in
  99. The City Council informs you that due to the accident at Chernobyl Power Station in the city of Pripyat the radioactive conditions in the vicinity are deteriorating. The Communist Party, its officials and the armed forces are taking necessary steps to combat this. Nevertheless, with the view to keep people as safe and healthy as possible, the children being top priority, we need to temporarily evacuate the citizens in the nearest towns of Kiev region.
  100. the town remains inhabited by ghosts where no live person should go
