Strangers

I see futures with people all the time.people on the street,sitting in coffee shops,getting eggs in a grocery store,on a walk with a dog.I see futures with people all the time,yet we do not meet,living unyielding parallel lines,becoming everlasting enigmas,with complexities unrevealedas our names pass each other by.I see futures with people all the time,but they vanish before I lock my eyes.Onbekenden……Strangers
