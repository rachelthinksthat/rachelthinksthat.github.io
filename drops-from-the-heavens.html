Drops From The Heavens

On a day that no one remembers, hot fire blew up from the ground. A strike of light out of the heavens set a spark that grew and grew. As the flame curled up to the sky in search of a kiss from the great expanse, warmth coursed through space giving comfort to an empty wasteland. A completeness the sky had never felt washed through on the breeze. The heavens looked down at the glimmering light smiling with ease. Reaching needing to embrace the sight to capture its affection, but the sky could only touch the earth in little drops of heaven. The heavens poured pieces to the ground to please the fire that blazed, as they rained down the brilliant light fluttered and flickered delighted. The more heaven that came the less light there was until there was no more, and then heavens cried out with a thundery shout for all the night to mourn.
