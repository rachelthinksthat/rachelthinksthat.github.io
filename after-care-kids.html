Imitation poem · LSU

after care kids line up on the wall

After Chen Chen’s “please take off shoes before entering…”

small children after school chaos the roars the screams the quiet down please

sit on the wall wait to be called the tick tick tock tock the clicks of their heels

the twiddling of their fingers the whispers to their friend and faces back and forth

no talking do not take out snacks do not put up your backpacks do not move

a sense of misery a feeling of neglect but you are loved and your parents will be here soon & home & smile & love but be quiet right now

and do not eat that candy waiting listening for the name to be called do not make a scene the teacher is yelling do not stand out become one with the wall while others scream and shout keep your hands to your self a game of red light green light the playground the goal but the wall is the start and the teacher is not saying green ants crawling on your skin the last to be signed in the light turns to green then go go go
