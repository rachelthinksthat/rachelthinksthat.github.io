Innovation proposal · UAL · April 2026

Entomology in Sport

A digital-first Read-to-Me book with an animated companion video, for Ladybird on Epic!. Ages 5 to 6 and Year 1 educators.

The idea

Imagine Mary M. Ward's cheerfully whimsical book Entomology in Sport brought to life as an educational digital reimagination. Our goal is to keep the illustration style and expand upon it to create a Read-to-Me/Read-by-Myself book with a subtly animated, gently narrated video book as a companion.

Educators and parents have a wide variety of tech to choose from these days. Platforms like Vooks and ABCmouse dominate the space with over 45M+ users for their gamified educational tools. What's largely missing is any sense of artistic or scientific heritage.

Our own digital platform for Ladybird is still growing, so by hosting Entomology in Sport on Epic!, we strategically give our audience a taste of what's to come. What that creates is an unusually low-risk way to test marketability before extending into print. The single-title digital pilot also acts as a way for us to gather real engagement data before committing to a full series.

Audience

Teachers of children aged 5 to 7 and the children themselves are both the target audience of this book. A reliable and safe plug-and-play educational tool helps alleviate feelings of being spread too thin with stressful lessons, while meeting National Curriculum science and literacy goals.

As a teacher, I'm feeling burnt out trying to teach 30 children to read at a time, and wish I had a way to engage them in independent learning while still facilitating their phonetical decoding capabilities and comprehension skills.

As a student, I don't think reading is fun and wish I could see the pictures move and hear the story so I can read better. I like to feel like I'm there, so sounds that match the characters would excite me!

Design choices

By using unobtrusive and story targeted sounds, we will aid early educators in helping to teach children book flow by guiding them on their journey through rhythms that break down words into easily decodable bits.

Intentionally using colors that relax children and promote calm will lead to an emotional state more conducive to learning and retaining information. We will also be using Verbena, a reader friendly font, that will also allow for children to easily interpret and break down words into smaller bits, especially with Epic!'s word-highlighting function to bring even greater emphasis.

MoSCoW

Must haveMulti-format functionality, modernized and condensed text, updated illustrations in the original style, subtle 2D puppet animation, calm narration
Should haveCharacter voice acting, ambient sounds, a quiz at the end, spotlights on facts
Could haveA physical book companion, a series of books
Won't haveGamification, overstimulating images or sounds, unfaithful retellings
