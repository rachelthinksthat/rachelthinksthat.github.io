The Truth Comes Out

Three scenes, one night

FADE IN.

1INT. KATE AND JEREMY’S HOUSE. NIGHT.1

The door opens and JEREMY (35) and KATE (32) walk through the door into the house. Jeremy stands by the kitchen counter as KATE moseys over to the couch.

JEREMY

There was another call from a University.

Kate has her back to him and sets down her purse on the arm of the couch. She looks up, sighs, and turns back around to look at Jeremy.

KATE

Are we doing this again?

JEREMY

It’s kind of hard to avoid when it keeps popping up.

Jeremy throws his jacket on the kitchen table, causing a MANILA FOLDER and some papers to fall on the ground. He walks to the glass bar cart, pours scotch into a crystal cup, then shoots back the liquor.

KATE

I don’t know what you want me to say at this point.

JEREMY

I don’t know, Kate, maybe that you actually give a shit about me and want to stay with me.

Kate ignores this statement and proceeds to cross her arms as she looks away silently with her gaze falling on the MANILA FOLDER and her eyes grow big.

She anxiously walks over to the kitchen table and hurriedly shuffles the fallen papers as Jeremy sets down his glass, walks to the table and interrupts her

JEREMY

I’ll get it.

Jeremy bends down and reaches to grab the MANILA FOLDER as Kate snatches it away from him.

KATE

I got it.

Kate and Jeremy pause and stare at each other in a stoney silence which makes Jeremy suspicious.

JEREMY

What’s wrong with you?

Jeremy grabs the stack of papers out of Kate’s hands including the MANILA FOLDER and notices that the return address on the folder says "SAVANNAH COLLEGE OF ART AND DESIGN" then looks up at Kate furiously.

JEREMY

Is there something you need to tell me?

Kate ignores the question, snatches the FOLDER from Jeremy’s hands, and walks away toward her studio while Jeremy rushes after her and cuts her off in the...

2INT. HALLWAY OF JEREMY AND KATE’S HOUSE - CONTINUOUS2

hallway with his arm outstretched in front of her studio door.

JEREMY

Just be honest with me, Kate.

KATE

There’s nothing more to talk about, Jeremy.

JEREMY

I need to know what’s going on here! You can’t shut me out of this.

KATE

It has nothing to do with you!

JEREMY

Are you kidding me Kate? You’re my wife this has everything to do with me.

Kate pushes past him, walks into her studio, and places the manila FOLDER on her desk close to her velvet green CHAIR.

3INT. KATE’S ART STUDIO - CONTINUOUS3

Jeremy turns and follows her into the studio finding moving boxes stacked around the room and paintings wrapped in packing material.

JEREMY

What the hell is this?

Kate picks up an opened BOTTLE of Merlot off her desk and uncorks it.

KATE

I accepted a job offer at SCAD. I didn’t tell you because I didn’t know what to say.

Kate takes a swig straight out of the BOTTLE and sets bottle back on her desk and turns to look at Jeremy who glares at her in disbelief.

JEREMY

So, what? Was I supposed to come home one day to find that you packed up all your things and left?

Jeremy leans against the door frame and looks away and Kate sits on her velvet green desk CHAIR and looks out of the rainy window behind her desk.

KATE

It’s not like that, Jeremy.

Jeremy flies across the room in rage and grabs the arms of the CHAIR. Kate flinches and turns sharply to meet Jeremy’s glaring eyes.

JEREMY

Then what is it like?!

Kate glares back at Jeremy, ducks her way under his arm and out of the chair.

KATE

It’s an amazing job opportunity that I have dreamed about for years, can’t you just be happy for me?

JEREMY

Happy? You expect me to be happy that you are literally abandoning me to move across the country to start a whole new life without me?

Jeremy furiously walks toward Kate’s messy workstation and shoves her boxes to the ground. Kate just stares at Jeremy in silence as he tears a painting off the wall and slings it across the room knocking the BOTTLE to the ground as he pushes down her easel putting a hole in the wall. Kate puts her face in her hands and cries.

KATE

I’m sorry I know I’m selfish but this is what I have to do! I’ll resent you if I stay here. I feel stuck and I just can’t live like this anymore. It’s not because I don’t love you it’s just because of me.

Jeremy looks at Kate incredulously as he yells

JEREMY

That’s bullshit, Kate. You never once cared about me, this is entirely about you not us and I am done with it! If you’re leaving I want a divorce. I can’t do this anymore.

KATE

No! How can you say that, every decision I’ve ever made has revolved around you, but now I have decided to do something for myself. We can make this work. We can make time to see each other every month or so. I love you, Jeremy. I don’t want to lose you.

JEREMY

I hate to break it to you, but "Love" doesn’t leave. Do what you want, just know the day you move out is the day I file for divorce.

Jeremy barges out of the room kicking a box out of his way and slamming the door loudly, which makes Kate jump.

Kate shudderingly stares at the door then looks around at the mess as tears well up in her eyes.

She lets out a pitiful wheeze as she slowly falls to the ground next to her boxes curling into a ball with her face resting next to the fresh wine stain and the now empty bottle.

She wipes the tears from her face as she sniffles and gazes up at her desk with her focus landing on the manila FOLDER.

FADE OUT.
