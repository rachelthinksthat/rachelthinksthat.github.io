Idiosyncrasies? Hmm…

Written for my first poetry writing class, where my classmates and I each wrote one about ourselves as an introduction to one another. This was mine.

Well, my least favorite thing on the ENTIRE planetis about half the size of my pinky.When I think about them I cringe,loathing fills my stomach,and spews out of me like vomit.They crawl around like they’re all innocent…BUT THEY’RE NOT AND I HATE THEM!The mere thought of them makes my whole body ache.I can feel their tiny little handssneaking up my spine,tickling my ears,inching along my collarbone,crawling down my arms making me quakeEuuuuughhhhThey are LITERALLY the bane of my existence,yet everyone loves them because they go through MeTaMoRpHoSiS.“Protect them” they say “They’ll be beautiful some day”.NOPE!I’m going to pound ‘em into the pavement until I physically break my legs,fully obliterating any evidence that they ever even existed,then I’ll never have to think about them AGAIN!Instead I’ll think about spoonsmy most favorite things in the WORLD.They make my heart explode into c o n f e t t i,spoons with their elegance,their grace,their evenly shining symmetry,their sequential slap ya mama sipping sensations,their… un-usefulness with spaghetti?OOOOOOOWEEEEEE!!!I would stalk spoons if I could but I’m not sure how…maybe, lurking through Williams Sonoma,sauntering over to the silverware section,where Jim stocks the silver spoonswhile I stalk the silver spoonscreeping upjust so I could…grip the handle…for a second!PHHEEEEEEWWWW!!!!Alright THAT’s it. I’ve shared my idiosyncrasies.I must be done,and don’t you DARE judge me harshly!We’re the same you and me!!We both have our dislikes and our likes as well,so now you know mine and you have yours to tell.
