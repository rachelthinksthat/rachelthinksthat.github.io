Adulting

Excerpts from the opening scenes

Excerpt one: the opening

FADE IN:

1EXT. DOWNTOWN BATON ROUGE - NIGHT1

Green Happy’s Irish Pub sign shines through the night.

The street bustles with drunk people. RILEY (22) wearing red lipstick takes a drag of an electric cigarette, she sits on a wire bench while, A COUPLE (man 28, woman 23) has an inaudible argument across the street.

TESSA (22) rushes down the sidewalk towards Riley. Her blonde hair looks like a lion’s mane from the humidity. She breathes winded from running.

TESSA

Sorry I’m late girl... Traffic was’n’sane on the bridge.

Riley smiles as she jumps up to hug Tessa. Squeezing her neck with a squeal of excitement.

RILEY

No worries! I’ve missed you it’s been way too long.

TESSA

I’ve missed you too. Where’s Kat?

Riley rolls her eyes and gestures to the COUPLE making out across the street.

RILEY

Andrew and Kathryn are ‘arguing’ again.

TESSA

What this time.

RILEY

God only knows.

Kathryn and Andrew’s make out across the street escalates.

TESSA

Leave room for Jesus!

Kathryn flips Tessa off while continuing to make out. Riley and Tessa bust out laughing.

Riley puts her arm around Tessa’s shoulder as they start walking towards the entrance to Happy’s

TESSA (CONT'D)

I mean I can hardly believe she’s still a virgin.

RILEY

I can hardly believe we all are.

Excerpt two: the piano bar

INT. HAPPY’S IRISH PUB FRONT BAR - LATER

Popcorn kernels and bar napkins litter the floor of the dingy pub. Bartenders in short plaid skirts, tall white knee socks, tight shirts, and ribbons in their hair pour beer from taps, fix drinks, and interact with customers.

Dart boards line a wall above wooden booths. People fill the bar drinking and shout to each other above the loud rap music.

Kathryn and Andrew enter the bar looking for Riley and Tessa continuing a conversation from earlier.

Kathryn seems annoyed but holds Andrew’s hand as they walk through the pub.

ANDREW

..all I’m saying is that your birthday’s tomorrow, and we’ve been dating for a while and I just want to give you a good present.

KATHRYN

You know how I feel about this. It’s not like I don’t want to I just can’t.

ANDREW

You keep saying that but I can tell you don’t mean it.

PIANO music fades in as they walk through a door into...

INT. HAPPY’S IRISH PUB PIANO BAR - CONTINUOUS

In the PIANO bar the loud rap music is drowned out by piano man. Kathryn stops Andrew by putting her hand on his chest.

KATHRYN

I love you, but you need to support my decisions.

Kathryn kisses Andrew lightly.

ANDREW

Okay.
