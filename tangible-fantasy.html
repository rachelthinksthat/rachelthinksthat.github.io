Tangible Fantasy

Eyes race across the tree line,where did he go? she thinks as she speeds through time.Shouting to the clouds to aid in her searchfind him he’s somewhere on this earth!Responding morphing from fluffy white,expanding glowing fiery bright,spinning and turningbrilliantly burning,suddenly a dragon with kaleidoscope eyesenormous wings stretch across the skies.In a blink the beast disappears,so she relies on what she hears.She gazes at the rustling treeslistening fervently for his screams.He chose this gameso he’s really to blame.Swinging swiftly from tree to treemonkeys swarming around with glee.Laughing and grinning from ear to earYou’ll never find this boy my dear!She watches as her beautiful beast fliesover the trees with occasional dives.Looking all around for their jungle born friendhurrying for the journey will soon end.A familiar gardenia bush comes into visionthe man emerges from where he was hidden.He gives a slight frown and a silent wavenow we arrive at the crackling driveway.Mom shouts “we’re home” dreams slowly fadedissolving my dragon ending my crusade.I stretch my arms out wide as I canthinking I can’t wait to play again.Mom opens the door and smiles with charmshe loops my backpack around my arm.Walking up the pathway hand in hand“did you have a good day at school today?”
