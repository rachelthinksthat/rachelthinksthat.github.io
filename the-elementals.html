Campaign proposal · January 2026

The Elementals: Find Your Element

Launch campaign for Love Ssega's debut children's book, Wanzu Sets Sail. Audience: parents of children aged 5 to 7.

The idea

Our goal is to give parents and kids 5-7 a wondrous experience. What better way than a family event and a new world of fantasy? Our author Love Ssega’s personality and musical talent gives his book a unique intrigue by making his fantasy world more than a book. His music draws our consumer into a real-world adventure of creativity for the whole family.

Stephanie, a mom of a 5-7 year old, who misses the times when storytime was easy and fun. Now that her kids are in school, screens are fun and reading is just homework. How can she bond with her child without having to stare at a screen together? Let’s make reading feel exciting again. Our campaign gives them an opportunity to enter a whole new world by Finding Their Element. Stephanie and her children make a great escape from screens and set out on a quest to Save Sealand.

A proposal for my MA, not a live campaign.

From Reel to pre-order

First we capture Stephanie’s attention on Instagram and YouTube through Reels and Shorts. A link in the caption leads her to our interactive Element Quiz, leading her to share her email to receive “Quest Reward Code”, and learn about our in-person event. Emailed newsletters and more posts on library or bookshop social channels will gently remind her by breaking through the mental chaos of parenting subtly.

Parents often co-watch screens with their kids, so a short playfully animated video featuring Love Ssega’s music invites them to pause and interact. They want to know their role in the story, so it’s easy to type in an email! Entertain the kids, save money, family time? A no brainer.

Stephanie comes across the Reel, clicks through to the quiz, receives a discount code, signs up by email, receives event reminders, attends the Elementals Quest event with her kids, and uses her Quest Reward Code to pre-order The Elementals.

Messaging

Using quest language makes the whole experience more exciting calling them to Find their Element, Save the Island, and Join the adventure. Playfully drawing them into the adventure, where they can discover new music, a fresh fantasy, and family fun.

Our Social posts call our consumer to begin a quest to find their element and help save the island at our big event. They can listen to the first chapter, hear music from the author, and participate in creative activities. Everyone can Find their Element and help save the Sealand.
