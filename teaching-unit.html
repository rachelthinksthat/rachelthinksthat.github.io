Teaching unit · 7th grade English

Community, Society, and Diversity: How do you fit in?

A four-week unit plan with a letter to parents, essential questions, a calendar and full lesson plans.

Letter to parents

Dear Parents,

As your children enter the 7th Grade they will begin to realize that they are not like everyone else. Everyone has their own special unique qualities that set them apart from others, and that give them their own special role within our community. As we go through this unit we will be exploring two books that emphasize differences rather than stifle them. These books will help your children understand that it is okay to be different, and that they do not have to try to be anyone other than themselves.

This unit we will read Akata Witch by Nnedi Okorafor and Seedfolks by Paul Fleischman. The books cover some sensitive subjects such as teen pregnancy, illness, and disabilities that I would like to make you aware of before beginning the unit. We will discuss what makes a community, what makes people diverse, what culture is, and what society is. As we go along your children will create their own representation of how they view these subjects in the form of comic book, short story, etc. I hope that you are as excited as I am to see what your children create!

Warmly, Rachel Jordan

Essential questions

  • What does it mean to fit into society?
  • What is culture?
  • Why is it important to understand each other's differences?
  • How can people work together despite their differences?
  • How can you use your differences as your strength in the real world?

Unit overview

After reading Akata Witch and Seedfolks you will evaluate how the characters' perspectives and cultures differ from each other and how they contribute to their community. As we read through each of the books you will be doing reader responses to the chapters we cover for each week, and create a journal entry about something you found in the book that you can relate to in your own life.

Our main project for this unit will be to create your own character who is different and show how they gain strength from their differences. You will be able to stretch your imagination and create some sort of representation of your character whether it be a poem, comic book, short story, children's book, etc.

Four weeks

Week 1What makes people different; society and diversity. Akata Witch chapters 1 to 15, discussion circles, a Four Corners activity, final project introduced.
Week 2Culture and community. A walk-through of other cultures, Akata Witch wrap-up and quiz, Seedfolks begins, group work to build a community with a common goal.
Week 3Comparing the two books. Seedfolks wrap-up, compare and contrast group work, a test, a lesson on citations and MLA format, project work day.
Week 4Peer review, rough draft, final project and presentations, a unit summary and a closing journal entry.

A lesson in full

Lesson 2: What is culture?

OpenerHow is your culture different from Sunny's?
I do (10 min)Briefly define terms while students follow along with a PowerPoint.
We do (45 min)Students walk in their discussion groups to tables with a different country at each one, spending 5 minutes at each table learning about each culture. I direct the rotation and ensure maximum efficiency. Groups then discuss what they learned, and we discuss the findings of each group as a class.
You do (5 min)Students write about their own culture.
Exit ticketWhy is it important to learn about other cultures?
