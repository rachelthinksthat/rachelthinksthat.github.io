Poem

Yellow.

I never thought much of yellow as a coloracting like a sidelined character, never selected.Yellow.Yellow sun,yellow dress,yellow flowers,yellow cake.A warm color,seizing happiness,siphoning memories,spilling light.Yellow.Yellow signs,yellow lights,yellow tape,yellow wallpaper.A cautionary color,marinating hesitation,manipulating apprehension,managing expectation.Yellow.Yellow eyes,yellow skin,yellow teeth,yellow bruise.A sickly unnatural color,welcoming fatigue,welling morbidly,whispering disaster.Why then do I only think of yellow?Summer sunshine.yellow.happy.Melodramatic madness.yellow.waiting.Worrisome woefulness.yellow.unwell.An all consuming essence, embroidering existence.invading brightly sunkissed dreams,milkily dripping turmeric eyes,wrenching twisting warming gutting.How can a color be so kind and so invasive all at once?
