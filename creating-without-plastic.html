Book proposal · Usborne

Creating Without Plastic

A sustainable craft activity book for readers aged 10 and up, with market, competition and profit and loss.

Overview

Creating Without Plastic will be an educational craft focused companion activity book to Life Without Plastic by Chantal Plamondon and Jay Sinha. The book will condense their tested and researched information into language accessible to kids, so they can build healthy habits for taking care of earth early! Combining essential knowledge on sustainability with fun crafting activities will curate interest and eco-friendly habits in children.

Activities may include charts to track their waste, such as tracking how many times they use their reusable water bottle or how many times they pack a lunch without plastic. Gardening skills, food growing activities, and crafts like making their own reusable beeswax foodsavers or making cutting boards can teach children how to avoid buying produce in plastic at grocery stores and limit single use plastics for food preparation and protection.

Schools all over the UK have made efforts to reduce their carbon footprint through encouraging their students to use reusable water bottles or bringing packed lunches that limit food packaging. Creating Without Plastic would be a beneficial addition to the classroom as a way for teachers to find ideas for getting children more involved in saving the planet.

An ideal release date would be in the spring leading into summer because parents are looking for activities to keep children occupied over summer break. Spring is also the time when most schools have book fairs, so this book could be highlighted as an educational and fun activity source for the summer.

Internal fit

Creating Without Plastic fits within both the Activity Book and Life Skills book sections. The sustainability books having to do with lifestyle on our roster generally fall below the 10+ age range, so this book fills a gap for reaching older kids about eco-friendly life skills.

Our activities books do not contain many craft components and steer more towards written activities or simple art activities, so having a more involved crafting and creating book that also happens to promote protecting the planet and reach older audiences would fill multiple gaps at once.

Unique selling points

  1. Inspires kids to find creative ways to live without plastic
  2. Fills a gap for sustainable craft activities aimed at higher age groups
  3. Strong educational focus appeals to schools and parents
  4. Promotes actively changing harmful habits to sustainable actions

Creating Without Plastic isn't just an educational book, it gives children hands-on experience trading plastic for eco-friendly substitutes. Let's make learning about the environment fun and interactive!
