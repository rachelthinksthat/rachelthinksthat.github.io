CVS/Pharmacy’s Genius Fame Stealing Advertisement

English 1001 · Louisiana State University

The CVS/Pharmacy advertisement in People Magazine displays several brand name products, Crest 3D Whitening strips, Olay Moisturizer, Venus Snap razor, and Cover Girl Mascara, along with two girls, a hashtag which connects the ad to the social media app, Twitter, and an MTV Video Music Awards (VMA) sponsored logo. The advertisement uses a collage-like formatting with girls posed in the bottom left corner and the products floating up and to the right of them. On the top of the advertisement in large words reads “MAKE THE GRADE WITH Chart-Topping Trends Celeb-worthy looks seen at the VMAs send you back to campus in style” (CVS/Pharmacy Advertisement, People). In the bottom right corner of the advertisement lays the CVS/Pharmacy logo. The CVS/Pharmacy advertisement in People Magazine uses the desirability of certain brand name products to increase their own popularity, two flawless-looking girls that entice the audience by appealing to their vanities and envy using the window effect, and eye-catching words and colors that pique the audience’s interest, to make customers flock to discover the convenience and variety within CVS/Pharmacy.

Different brand name products associated with beauty, such as Crest, Olay, Cover Girl, and Venus, are displayed on the CVS/Pharmacy advertisement, which advertises the products and gives credibility to the store’s endless supply of necessities. It may seem as though the use of other products’ popularity is cheating in the advertisement world, but for a convenience store it is genius. By associating themselves with popular personal appearance products, CVS/Pharmacy increases their own popularity and desirability. The products chosen, such as whitening strips, moisturizer, mascara, and a razor, appeal to the viewer’s constant need for attractiveness. All of these products were selected to be displayed because they are expendable and considered necessities to the public. Displaying the products on their advertisement will make the viewer associate the products with CVS/Pharmacy, which as a result will cause the viewer to subliminally choose CVS/Pharmacy the next time they purchase that product.

The personal appearance products are accompanied by two gorgeous girls posed in a modelesque manner, which make the viewer self-conscious and overtake them with envy. The girls in the advertisement employ window effect by showing the audience what they could become. The way that the girls pose makes their physique look effortless, and as today’s world calls it, ‘hipster’. The girls are young and carrying books which relates to the vain ones who are fretting about their next look of the day and who still need to purchase school supplies. The books they carry add to CVS/Pharmacy’s wide array of products supplied within the store, and further captivate College girls’ attention with a future need. CVS/Pharmacy uses the girls in the advertisement to show that the result of coming in to the store and purchasing their products is flawlessness and preparedness.

Above the flawless girls are huge pink and black words that ignite the need for beauty even further; even the hashtag suggests an attainable “rocking” look and the VMAs advertisement implies that the viewer has an entourage which gives them a sense of importance. The usage of words such as “Classic”, “Chart-Topping”, “Campus Bombshell”, and “Celeb-worthy” all suggest that the viewer will be the envy of all others if they purchase products from CVS/Pharmacy. The colors, pink and black, were specifically chosen because pink is associated with girls and black is mysterious. The aforementioned words often identify important and beautiful people, which will captivate the viewer’s interest. The Twitter hashtag spreads the word about CVS/Pharmacy’s large supply of products that give you #LOOKSTHATROCK. Using the hashtag is both a good advertising strategy and grabs the interest of girls who use Twitter. The VMAs advertisement appeals to the girls need for importance and fame and also gives credibility to CVS/Pharmacy as if to say their products make people VMA ready.

The techniques used in the CVS/Pharmacy advertisement in People Magazine including memorable words and ties to Twitter and the VMAs, brand name products, and flawless girls increase CVS/Pharmacy’s credibility and reliability for a variety of products. The advertisement makes the store memorable and draws in college girls who want to be noticed. The advertisement convinces college girls to associate the beauty they strive for with CVS/Pharmacy, and as the need for flawlessness arises, they will remember where they can go to improve their image.
